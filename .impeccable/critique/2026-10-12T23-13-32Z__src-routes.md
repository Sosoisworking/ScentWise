## Design Specificity Verdict
LLM: logic and copy are product-specific (three-state note chips with live counts, vibes, "Matches N of M" with highlighted notes, Canada-first retailer tiers with landed-cost rules, "Scent has no gender" voice); the visual system is still template-shaped (centred Playfair H1 over rounded soft-shadow cards, pills for everything, one blurred gradient). Missed chances: no visual language for scent families, landed-cost rules are static prose rather than a calculator.
Deterministic scan: CLI 0 findings (detector verified working on a seeded file). Browser overlay on 6 pages, both themes: 0 low-contrast; true positives are cream-palette (light only), radial-spotlight-glow (.scent-aura), footer legal line ~164 chars, a few ~91-104 char paragraphs (retailers intro, author bio), em-dash advisory (brand taglines). False positives: cramped-padding on min-h-11 buttons, clipped-overflow on /brands main. No horizontal overflow at 375px.

## Overall Impression
The flow mechanics and honesty of the explanations are now strong. The biggest remaining risk sits at the end of the journey: the ranking rule can put fragrances that contradict explicit answers (Men's bottles for Women, medium wear for Long) near the top, and the card copy doesn't admit the miss.

## What's Working
- Scoring explained, not hidden: "Matches N of M of your notes" with highlighted pyramid notes, ranking rule under the heading, weights on About.
- Flow mechanics: URL steps with working Back/Forward, focus to step heading, announced step count, Undo on restart, one-answer edits, shareable results with a shared-link banner, session persistence.
- Canada-first retailer guide and a careful token system: tiered shops with checked dates and sources, worked landed-cost example, AA contrast in both themes.

## Priority Issues
- [P1] Results can contradict explicit answers, and card copy covers for it. Reproduced: Women + Fall + Date night + Long + Rose/Jasmine/Peony + Under $100 ranks Club de Nuit Intense Man (men's) #2 and Fierce (men's) #4; without the price filter, #1 and #2 are medium wear. Cause: matched-note count sorts before fit (scoring.ts), a 20-point threshold reachable on gender/longevity alone, and an unconditional "picked for your season and occasion" (FragranceCard.tsx:98,109). Fix: treat Women/Men as a filter or demote mismatches below all matches; add a fit row that shows misses; only claim season/occasion when true. Command: /impeccable harden, then /impeccable clarify.
- [P1] On mobile, results and forward actions are buried and floating controls cover options: first result at y=826 on an 812px screen; step 5's "Refine by notes" at y≈2,270; floating Back/Restart overlap option labels on steps 2-5; scroll-to-top covers the Sephora button. Fix: pinned bottom bar for steps 2-5, collapse answers into one editable sentence and price into a compact control, show matched notes with a "Full pyramid" disclosure. Command: /impeccable adapt (with layout).
- [P2] Notes step overloaded, counts mislead, avoid gesture hidden: ~95 targets; counts use the whole catalogue and go up as more notes are loved; avoid is the hidden second tap; Clear has no undo. Fix: lead with vibes, count against the pool that fits earlier answers, explicit Love/Avoid mode, Undo on Clear. Command: /impeccable distill.
- [P2] Price labels disagree with prices: midpoint banding yields "$200+ · CA$165–$260" and Under $100 returns CA$55–$110 bottles. Fix: drop the band from the badge, filter on the low end or call it "typically under $100", explain or drop badge colours. Command: /impeccable clarify.
- [P2] The site breaks a few of its own trust rules: Gmail as the only contact (the retailer guide's own warning sign); "Every fragrance house. One place."; Editor's Picks/Featured without criteria; seasonal "10 great..." articles mostly men's bottles under neutral titles; FragranceBuy as the unexplained default buy link. Command: /impeccable clarify.
